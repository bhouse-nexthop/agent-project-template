# Candidates

Follow-ups, ideas and findings that surfaced during work. Nothing here runs: the owner prioritises, and a chosen
candidate moves to the WORKLIST Queue (RULES rule 13). A finding outside the project's goal gets one line in its
record file at most, not an entry here.

Format: `- C<n> [area] **title** (found <YYYY-MM-DD> by <item>): what, why it matters, rough cost. Detail: <file>`

- C1 [area] **<title>** (found <YYYY-MM-DD> by <item>): <what, why it matters, rough cost>.
